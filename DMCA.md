## 1. Zero Multimedia / Non-Hosting Guarantee

1. **Text-Only Content**: All data files hosted in this repository consist exclusively of plain-text subtitle files in standard formats:
   - SubRip (`.srt`)
   - Web Video Text Tracks (`.vtt`)
   - Advanced SubStation Alpha (`.ass`)
2. **No Video or Audio Streaming**: This repository does **not** host, embed, buffer, proxy, or stream any copyrighted video files (e.g., `.mp4`, `.mkv`, `.avi`), audio files (e.g., `.mp3`, `.aac`), or live media playlists (e.g., `.m3u8`, `.mpd`).
3. **No Circumvention Tools**: This project contains **no** digital rights management (DRM) circumvention software, decryption keys, stream-ripping utilities, or torrent tracker files. It strictly adheres to Section 1201 of the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act and the WIPO Copyright Treaty.
4. **Independent Volunteer Localization**: Subtitle text entries are independent amateur fan-translations and localization manuscripts created by volunteer contributors for the Central Asian Kazakh-speaking community.

## 2. Accessibility & Transformative Fair Use (17 U.S.C. § 107)

This project is maintained for non-commercial, non-profit public benefit under the **Fair Use doctrine** of international copyright law and United States Code (17 U.S.C. § 107):

- **Accessibility for Deaf and Hard of Hearing (DHH)**: Many licensed media releases in the Republic of Kazakhstan and Central Asia lack native Kazakh closed captions. These subtitle files serve an essential accessibility purpose, enabling hearing-impaired citizens to understand and enjoy foreign artistic works alongside legally acquired physical or digital video copies.
- **Linguistic and Cultural Preservation**: The project promotes the modernization and terminological development of the Kazakh language (a Turkic language spoken by over 16 million people) in contemporary arts and cinematic media.
- **Transformative Educational Value**: The translations serve as comparative linguistic texts for academic research in Japanese-to-Kazakh translation, syntax, and computational linguistics.
